About us

Where crypto natives meet TradFi discipline

Campsor Capital is dedicated to generating risk-adjusted returns by managing a relative-value fund investing in crypto assets. It targets superior returns for investors through a semi-systematic approach that exploits market discontinuities whilst minimizing directional exposures and counterparty risk.

We aim to achieve consistent absolute returns within a meticulously defined risk framework.

Founded by the marriage of crypto natives with veterans from TradFi, our team brings extensive experience from prominent Wall Street institutions such as Deutsche Bank, Banca Intesa, and MUFG, as well as leading hedge funds. This expertise is enhanced by a robust academic foundation in econometrics, mathematics, and engineering.

Our fund

The Fund

01

Investment objectives

Campsor aims to achieve consistent, stable, and absolute returns by leveraging our diverse skillset and a disciplined risk management style. The fund targets double-digit returns with a volatility of 15–20%.

02

Our approach

The investment strategy is based on two pillars: monetization of dislocations across platforms and products; and dynamic allocation to market-neutral opportunities in innovative venues and protocols including DeFi.

Campsor aims at minimizing risks through a robust risk management approach and selection of tier-one platforms and service providers. Our investment process is data-driven and semi-systematic, allowing us to make objective decisions and execute trades efficiently.

Risk framework

The Hedge

Risk management

Traditional risk management approach pairing well with the innovative nature of crypto-assets, using data driven models and systematic scenario analysis to quantify exposures, preserve portfolio diversification and hedge unwanted risks.

Proprietary infrastructure

Combining our quantitative and DevOps expertise, we have developed advanced analytics and execution systems that power multi-venue trading with minimal slippage and efficient liquidity sourcing across spot, derivatives, and DeFi markets.

Counterparty protection

Pioneering counterparty credit risk mitigation via triparty agreements, where the collateral is held by a regulated trusted third-party custodian, reducing credit exposure.
